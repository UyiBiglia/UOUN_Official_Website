Library

University Library

Your gateway to knowledge, research, and academic success.

The Academia University Library is dedicated to supporting the teaching, learning, and research needs of our community. Whether you're looking for a quiet place to study, access to digital resources, or assistance with your research, we're here to help you succeed.

Library Services
Physical Library

Access our extensive collection of print books, journals, and reference materials.

E-Library

Access thousands of e-books, journals, and databases from anywhere, anytime.

Research Support

Get help with literature searches, citation management, and research methodology.

Borrowing & Returns

Easy borrowing, renewals, and returns with our efficient circulation system.

Digital Resources
E-books

Access over 50,000 e-books across all disciplines, available 24/7.

Academic Journals

Access thousands of peer-reviewed journals from leading publishers worldwide.

Research Databases

Explore specialized databases for in-depth research in your field of study.

Online Learning

Access tutorials, video lectures, and self-paced learning modules.

Library Facilities
Reading Areas

Quiet study zones and comfortable reading spaces for focused work.

Computer/Internet Access

High-speed internet and computer workstations for student use.

Group Study Spaces

Bookable rooms for collaborative learning and group projects.

Research Areas

Dedicated spaces for graduate students and faculty research.
