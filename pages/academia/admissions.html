Your Academic Journey Starts Here

Take the first step toward a rewarding future at U.O.U.N. Our admission process is simple and transparent.

How to Apply

1

Submit Application

Complete the online application form with your personal details, academic history, and preferred programme of study. Ensure all information provided is accurate before submitting.

2

Send Documents

Upload clear copies of your O'Level results, UTME result slip, birth certificate, passport photograph, and other required documents. Your application will not be processed until all documents are received.

3

Interview Process

Shortlisted candidates will be invited for a screening interview. This may be conducted in person or online depending on your location and programme.

4

Receive Decision

Successful applicants will receive an official admission letter via email and the university portal. Admitted students must accept their offer and pay the acceptance fee before the stated deadline.

Admission Requirements

Academic Records

Applicants must possess a minimum of five O'Level credit passes including English Language and Mathematics, obtained in not more than two sittings. Direct Entry candidates must present A'Level, ND, or HND certificates as applicable.

Recommendation Letters

Two letters of recommendation are required — one from your secondary school principal or previous institution and one from a reputable member of society who can attest to your character.

Personal Statement

Write a 200-word personal statement explaining why you chose your programme, your career goals, and how U.O.U.N will help you achieve them.

Standardized Tests

All applicants must present a valid UTME result with a minimum score of 180. Candidates applying for postgraduate programmes must provide evidence of previous academic performance and any required professional certifications.
