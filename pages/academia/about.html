Our Story

Building Our Students for a Better Tomorrow

Founded in 2005, U.O.U.N began with three faculties and 200 students. Named after its visionary founder, Uyienminoseye Okodugha, the university has grown into one of Nigeria's most promising institutions of higher learning, driven by a commitment to academic excellence and character development.

    • Personal details (full name, date of birth, gender)

  3. How We Use Your Information
    • Process your admission application

    • Accreditation bodies for verification purposes

    • Rejected application data is deleted after one year
  8. Your Rights
