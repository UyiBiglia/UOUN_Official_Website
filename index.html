Convocation Ceremony 2026: Celebrating Our Graduates

September 4, 2026 10:00 AM Main Auditorium

The University is proud to announce its annual Convocation Ceremony, a grand celebration of our graduating students. This momentous occasion marks the culmination of years of hard work, dedication, and academic excellence.

The ceremony will feature:

  • Conferment of degrees and diplomas
  • Presentation of awards to outstanding students
  • Keynote address by a distinguished guest speaker
  • Special recognition of first-class graduates
  • Alumni induction ceremony

Families, friends, and well-wishers are cordially invited to join us in celebrating this remarkable milestone. The event promises to be a memorable day filled with pride, joy, and inspiration.

Graduands are required to complete their clearance and collect their academic gowns from their respective faculty offices before the ceremony date.

For more information, please contact the Office of the Registrar.

New Hostel Complex Commissioned — More Accommodation for Students

August 5, 2026 11:00 AM New Hostel Site, Campus East

The University is pleased to announce the official commissioning of a new state-of-the-art hostel complex, expanding accommodation capacity for our growing student population.

The newly completed facility features:

  • 500 fully furnished rooms with modern amenities
  • 24/7 electricity supply with backup generators
  • High-speed internet connectivity
  • Modern kitchenettes and common rooms
  • Round-the-clock security surveillance
  • Dedicated study areas on each floor
  • Accessible facilities for students with special needs

Speaking at the commissioning ceremony, the Vice-Chancellor emphasized the university's commitment to providing a conducive living and learning environment for all students.

"The welfare of our students is a top priority. This new facility reflects our dedication to creating a home away from home where students can focus on their academic pursuits," the Vice-Chancellor said.

Room allocation will be done through the student portal on a first-come, first-served basis. Students are advised to apply early to secure their spot.

For more information, contact the Student Affairs Department.

New Dress Code Policy Takes Effect This Semester

June 6, 2026 9:00 AM University Senate Chamber

The University Senate has approved a new dress code policy for all students, effective from the beginning of this semester. The policy is designed to promote decency, professionalism, and a sense of pride within the university community.

Key highlights of the new policy include:

  • Formal or smart casual attire for lectures and academic activities
  • Strictly prohibited: ripped jeans, sleeveless tops for male students, and revealing clothing
  • Department-specific dress requirements for certain programmes (e.g., lab coats for science students, clinical attire for medical students)
  • Proper identification cards must be worn at all times within campus premises
  • Traditional or cultural attire is encouraged on designated days

The Dean of Student Affairs emphasized that the policy is not meant to suppress individual expression but to prepare students for professional life after graduation.

"As future leaders, our students must understand that dressing appropriately is part of professionalism. This policy prepares them for the expectations of the workplace," the Dean stated.

Students are advised to familiarize themselves with the full guidelines available on the student portal and at their faculty offices.

Non-compliance with the dress code policy may result in denial of access to lecture halls and other university facilities.

For clarifications, contact the Student Affairs Department.
